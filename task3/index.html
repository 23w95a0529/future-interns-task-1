Our story

Started at a college canteen counter, still run the same way.

Tumbler & Leaf began in 2019 as a small counter run by the Rao family, three streets away from where the café now stands. The recipe for the decoction hasn't changed — it still takes eight hours, filtered the traditional way, every single morning before opening.

We're not trying to be a big chain. We're trying to be the place you stop at on your way to work, and the place you bring visitors when you want them to taste Hyderabad properly.
